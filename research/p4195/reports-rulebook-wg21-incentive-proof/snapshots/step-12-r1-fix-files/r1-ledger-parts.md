## Foundation changes

- ax:prefer. Old: "Given a choice, and other things equal, each participant, sponsor, national body, and user prefers the option that brings it more of what it values, sooner, at less cost to it, and weighs a gain in one value against a loss in another on one scale of its own. For this proof, a participant values adoption of the proposals they support, the interests of their sponsor if they have one, their standing, keeping any chair or convenor post they hold, their time and money, and a good result for users; a sponsor values its interests, which can depend on particular features of a design; a national body values the outcomes its national position supports and, where it has none yet, the interests concerned at its national level; and a user values the features they use or would use, how well those features work, and their own time and money." New: "Other things equal, each participant, sponsor, national body, and user prefers whatever brings it more of what it values, sooner and at less cost to it, weighing gains against losses on one scale of its own. For this proof, a participant values adoption of the proposals they support, the interests of their sponsor if they have one, their standing, keeping any chair or convenor post they hold, their time and money, and a good result for users; a sponsor values its interests, which can depend on particular features of a design; a national body values the outcomes its national position supports, the interests concerned at its national level where it has no position yet, and its members' time; and a user values the features they use or would use, how well those features work, and their own time and money."
- ax:support. Old: "A participant who votes Against a colleague's proposal cannot rule out that the colleague will support the participant's own proposals less readily. A Neutral vote, or not voting, gives the colleague no more reason for this than an Against vote does."
