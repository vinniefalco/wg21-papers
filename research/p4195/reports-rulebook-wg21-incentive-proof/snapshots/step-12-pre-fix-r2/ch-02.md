## 2. Who decides, and with what moves

1. **Proposition.** The participants present decide a design's content: chairs call consensus on their votes, counted under the 2:1 norm, in the design rooms and the study groups that send them proposals. SD-4 assigns no one to rule on a design's quality, wording rooms check only that wording expresses the design, and national bodies vote on each balloted draft as a whole. *[proved by l:design-rooms-forward, l:sg-initial-poll, l:wording-expresses, l:plenary-adopts, l:nb-whole-draft]* *[from f:one-vote, f:two-to-one, f:no-merit-ruling, d:follows-norm]* {#p:who-decides}
   1. **Lemma.** The design rooms select designs, and a design room sends one to the wording rooms once its chair determines that it has the room's general consensus. *[from f:vehicle, f:design-forward, d:rooms]* {#l:design-rooms-forward}
   1. **Lemma.** A study group produces the initial proposal a design room starts from, and its chair may poll alternatives side by side to see which has most consensus. *[from f:sg-initial-proposal, f:chair-polls, f:multiple-polls, d:rooms]* {#l:sg-initial-poll}
   1. **Lemma.** A wording room sends wording to plenary once its chair determines a consensus that the wording correctly expresses the intended design, and sends design questions back to the design room. *[from f:spec-subgroups, f:wording-forward, f:wording-refer, d:rooms]* {#l:wording-expresses}
   1. **Lemma.** Plenary decides, by the convenor's consensus call, whether to adopt each change the design and wording rooms recommend, and leaves design to the design rooms. *[from f:plenary-owns, f:plenary-no-design, d:rooms]* {#l:plenary-adopts}
   1. **Lemma.** A national body's ballot vote counts for or against a whole draft. SD-4 reserves No for blocking every part of the work, and on a final draft ISO bars a Yes conditional on changes. *[from f:enquiry-count, f:iso-ballot, f:ballot-no, f:no-conditional-yes, d:national-body]* {#l:nb-whole-draft}
1. **Proposition.** Each person in a subgroup's room has one vote, guests a convenor invites included, while no user votes in any WG21 poll or in the ballots on its drafts. *[from f:one-vote, f:guests, f:all-nbs-vote, f:iso-ballot, d:participant, d:user, d:national-body, f:plenary-polls]* {#p:who-votes}
1. **Proposition.** The players are result-seeking authors, design-bound authors, regulars, study group chairs, design room chairs, the convenor, national bodies, sponsors, and users. The proof takes as given SD-4's text, the convenor's identity, appointments, study groups, and guest invitations, and the choices of wording room chairs, guests, non-regular participants, and the direction group. *[from p:who-decides, p:who-votes, d:settled-pattern, d:author, d:result-seeking, d:design-bound, d:regular, d:rooms, d:national-body, d:sponsor, d:user]* {#m:players}
1. **Proposition.** Each player has these moves under the rules. *[proved by m:moves-author, m:moves-regular, m:moves-chair, m:moves-convenor, m:moves-nb, m:moves-sponsor, m:moves-user]* {#m:moves}
   1. **Lemma.** An author can submit papers, including papers that each propose part of a design, and present them or have a qualified and prepared presenter do so. An author can also revise them and ask the chair for a poll or the design room for a ship vehicle, which the chair and the design rooms decide. *[proved by inf:part-is-addition, inf:part-on-agenda]* *[from f:no-paper, f:present-required, f:invested, f:chair-polls, f:vehicle, d:author]* {#m:moves-author}
      1. A part of a design proposed on its own is a design addition, which SD-4 requires to have a paper. *[from f:no-paper]* {#inf:part-is-addition}
      1. Like every paper in the mailings since the previous meeting, one proposing part of a design is on the agenda, and SD-4 names no step for reviewing the parts together. *[from f:agenda-papers, f:no-parts-step]* {#inf:part-on-agenda}
   1. **Lemma.** A regular can attend or not, study a paper or not, speak, and vote Favor, Neutral, or Against, or not at all. A regular can also write a paper, raise a concern privately with a chair, and ask their national body to take a national position. *[from ax:cost, ax:attention, f:negative-welcome, f:one-vote, f:not-vote, f:no-paper, f:followup-papers, f:private-concern, f:escalate-nb, d:regular]* {#m:moves-regular}
   1. **Lemma.** Any chair can choose the polls, order the agenda by SD-4's priorities, re-poll after discussion, ask Against voters for reasons, and allow limited exploration of alternatives. Each chair can call consensus on its room's polls, and design room chairs can also time-box a new proposal or delay one a meeting for a paperless rival. *[from f:chair-polls, f:prioritization, f:direction-group, f:agenda-papers, f:timebox, f:two-to-one, f:against-reasons, f:explore-alternatives, f:design-forward, f:wording-forward, f:no-wait-alternatives, f:sg-initial-proposal, d:rooms]* {#m:moves-chair}
   1. **Lemma.** The convenor can appoint chairs, form study groups, invite a guest for up to two meetings, take any plenary polls, and make plenary consensus calls. In plenary the convenor can ask whether Against votes are personal or national body positions. *[from f:chairs-appointed, f:sg-formation, f:guests, f:plenary-owns, f:plenary-consent, f:plenary-nb, f:plenary-polls]* {#m:moves-convenor}
   1. **Lemma.** A national body can appoint experts, vote Yes, No, or abstain on ballots, comment, state national positions in plenary, appeal, and object in SC22 and JTC 1 to off-agenda action. It can vote on or confirm the convenor's appointment and, with at least one other P-member, have a draft discussed when they disagree with the SC22 chair's decision. *[from f:experts-appointed, f:iso-ballot, f:all-nbs-vote, f:nb-abstain, f:comments-national, f:plenary-nb, f:agenda-notice, f:appeal, f:respond-comments, f:convenor-term]* {#m:moves-nb}
   1. **Lemma.** A sponsor can pay for participation and choose whom and how many it pays, and a sponsor that is a category C liaison has representatives who act for it. Unless it is an ISO member body or a liaison organization, a sponsor has no vote of its own in WG21's polls or in the ballots on WG21's drafts. *[from d:sponsor, ax:who-pays, f:no-sponsor-cap, f:one-vote, f:experts-appointed, f:experts-personal, f:iso-ballot, f:all-nbs-vote, d:national-body]* {#m:moves-sponsor}
   1. **Lemma.** A user casts no vote in WG21's polls or ballots but can give feedback on a TS or white paper or write a paper a qualified and prepared participant presents. A user can also raise their interests with a national body, which ISO asks to take account of all interests concerned at the national level, or become a participant. *[proved by inf:user-joins]* *[from p:who-votes, f:ts-feedback, f:present-required, f:iso-early, d:user]* {#m:moves-user}
      1. A person becomes a participant once a national body or category A liaison appoints them as an expert, they are registered, and they attend a WG21 meeting. *[from d:participant, f:experts-appointed, f:registered, d:national-body]* {#inf:user-joins}

In plain terms: the participants present decide a design's content through consensus calls on their votes under the 2:1 norm, and SD-4 assigns no one to rule on a design's quality. No user votes in WG21's polls or ballots, and a sponsor has no vote of its own there unless it is an ISO member body or a liaison organization. The players are result-seeking and design-bound authors, regulars, study group and design room chairs, the convenor, national bodies, sponsors, and users, each with the moves the rules provide.
