# Rules check: foundation draft

Checked the foundation draft (chapter 1, "Starting points") against the rule extract (SD-4 in full, plus the extracted clauses of ISO/IEC Directives Part 1). Scope: all.

Result: 6 findings, all misquotes. No wrong-section, unsupported rule claim, or unsupported absence claim findings.

Coverage: 92 Facts (17 section-header Facts, 70 quoting Facts, 5 absence Facts), plus each Axiom and Definition that describes a rule. All 70 quoting Facts match the extract word for word and sit under the section they cite. The 6 misquotes are cuts: the words match, but a truncation or elision drops a condition, an exception, or a referent, so the Fact reads as a different rule from the one in the extract. Every replacement quote below has been matched word for word against the extract, and none crosses a page number or copyright line.

## Absence searches

Each absence Fact was checked by searching the whole named document for its own terms plus synonyms (case-insensitive, word stems where shown).

- f:no-reason-record (SD-4, whole document). Searched: record, minute, reason, answer, tally, objection, rationale, disposition, notes, summar, explain, justif, why, respond, response. Nearest hits: the chair may ask Against voters for "their reasons" ("Subgroup responsibility and polls"); in plenary, a subgroup chair may be asked to summarize "the reason for the decision of the subgroup" ("Closing plenary session responsibility and polls"); TS ballot comments "must be formally responded to with a Disposition of Comments paper" ("Technical Specifications (TS) and White Papers"); "All meetings must be minuted." ("Other topics"). None requires the record of a poll to show how an objection was answered. Holds.
- f:iso-no-reason-record (ISO/IEC Directives Part 1, 1.8.2, 2.5.7, 5.5, then the rest of the extracted ISO text). Searched: reason, rationale, justif, why, basis, because, ground, explain, summar, observation, register, record, minute, documented, in written form. In the three named clauses: 1.8.2 d) and e) require points of view to be "adequately summarized" and decisions "made available in written form"; 2.5.7 requires the leadership to "register the opposition"; 5.5 requires that "meeting outcomes are properly documented and shared". Elsewhere: 2.6.2 and 2.7.2 require a National Body voting negatively to "state the reasons", and 2.6.5 requires the enquiry report to "state the decision of the Chair of the committee" and include the secretariat's "observations" on each comment. None requires a record of why consensus was judged to exist. The item's description of the three clauses as governing how decisions, minutes, and oppositions are recorded is also supported. Holds.
- f:no-sponsor-cap (SD-4 and all extracted ISO text). Searched: sponsor, employ, compan, organization, organisation, firm, corporat, affiliat, fund, paid, pay, maximum, limit, cap, per person. Nearest hits: 1.12.1 lets the committee "decide upon the maximum number of Experts appointed by each P-member and liaison organization" (a permission to cap by appointing body, not by payer); 5.3.1 lets "voluntary sponsors" fund meeting facilities. Holds.
- f:no-merit-ruling (SD-4, whole document). Searched: rule, rules, ruling, decide, arbit, correct, authority, judg, determin, settle, adjudic, merit, verdict, select, opinion, verif, dispute, own, owns. Nearest hits: design subgroups "own selecting and recommending feature designs"; wording is forwarded on "a general consensus of the specification subgroup, as determined by the subgroup chair, that its specification correctly expresses the intended design"; an editing committee checks "that all approved changes were applied correctly"; the direction group gives an advisory "opinion on any specific proposal"; the conduct slide says "Uphold the agreed dispute resolution processes". Each is a consensus call, an advisory opinion, or an editing check, not a ruling on which side of a technical dispute is correct. Holds.
- f:no-concern-record (SD-4, whole document). Searched: privat, confidential, record, minute, concern, notes, wiki, log, file, track. Nearest hits: "they should share their concern privately and in advance with the subgroup chair" (no recording step follows); "All meetings must be minuted." (covers meetings, not a private message to a chair); step 6 of "How to report a potential technical concern", "File the issue or write the paper" (a step for the person reporting a technical problem, not a duty to record a concern raised privately with a chair). Holds.

## Passed, no change required

- ax:who-pays: the claim that a sponsor "cannot attend, speak, or vote in the participant's place" fits SD-4 "In subgroup polls, each person in the room can cast one vote." and ISO 1.12.2 "Experts not registered to a WG in the ISO GD or the IEC EMS, as appropriate, shall not participate in its work."
- d:positions: SD-4 capitalizes "Strongly Favor, Weakly Favor, Neutral, Weakly Against, and Strongly Against" (subgroup 5-way polls) and "Favor, Against, and Neutral" (plenary 3-way polls); the 2:1 norm restates f:two-to-one; consensus is "as determined by the subgroup chair" or "as determined by the Convener".
- d:rooms, d:participant, d:national-body: match SD-4's design subgroups (EWG and LEWG) and specification subgroups (CWG and LWG), ISO 1.12.2's registration rule, and SD-4's SC 22 P-member national bodies.
- The 17 section-header Facts each name the section or clause their child Facts come from.
- f:iso-register: verbatim, and nothing it states conflicts with the extract. Its cuts do leave "If this is not the case" and "In such cases" without the 2.5.7 sentences they point to, which are about the leadership's assessment of whether an opposition is sustained by an important part of the concerned interest. Optional tightening only.
- ax:schedule: the three-year schedule is not in the extract, but the item doesn't attribute it to SD-4 or the Directives, and the kit allows it as a structural fact.
- The source titles "How to report a potential technical concern" and "Escalating a serious ... objection" are shortened forms of the full SD-4 headings and point to the right sections.
