## Findings

f:iso-no-answer | unsupported absence claim | Clause 2.6.5 holds a second written-answer requirement besides the one the item excepts: the full report on an enquiry ballot "shall" "d) include the observations of the secretariat of the committee on each of the comments submitted." That is a written answer to every enquiry comment, including those behind negative votes, and "observation" is not among the searched terms. No other listed clause adds one: 2.6.4 has a group "at a meeting, address any comments received", 2.7.5 records final-draft comments as "noted for future consideration", and 2.7.7 refers a failed final draft back "for reconsideration". | Write "Apart from 2.6.5's required responses and observations on all comments received, no ISO/IEC Directives Part 1 clause listed here requires a written answer to a working group or ballot objection" and add observation to the searched terms. l:iso-comment-answers already states both requirements.

m:moves-chair | unsupported rule claim | SD-4 ("Proposals and papers") limits this discretion in time: "At early discussion phases, subgroup chairs have discretion to allow limited exploration of alternatives to an active proposal to decide whether a second paper is warranted, or to discuss broader prerequisite topics that are exposed by a paper under discussion." The same section says "we do not significantly delay progress on concrete proposals in order to wait for alternative proposals we might get in the future." The lemma gives the move to any chair at any stage. | "and allow early, limited exploration of alternatives" (the sentence stays at 27 words). l:checks-alternatives has the same gap.
