## 2. Where decisions are made

1. **Proposition.** A design enters the working draft only after a design room forwards it, a wording room forwards its wording, and plenary approves it. *[proved by l:path-plenary-owns, l:path-plenary-adopts, l:path-design-forward, l:path-wording-forward]* {#p:path}
   1. **Lemma.** Plenary owns approval of adding a proposal and its wording to a working draft. *[from f:plenary-owns, d:rooms]* {#l:path-plenary-owns}
   1. **Lemma.** The changes plenary decides whether to adopt are those that the design rooms and the wording rooms recommend. *[from f:plenary-owns, f:vehicle, f:spec-subgroups, d:rooms]* {#l:path-plenary-adopts}
   1. **Lemma.** A design room recommends a design by forwarding it to the wording rooms once the room reaches a general consensus on the design and its ship vehicle. *[from f:vehicle, f:design-forward, d:rooms]* {#l:path-design-forward}
   1. **Lemma.** A wording room recommends a design's wording by forwarding it to plenary once the room reaches a general consensus that the wording correctly expresses the intended design. *[from f:spec-subgroups, f:wording-forward, d:rooms]* {#l:path-wording-forward}
1. **Proposition.** Plenary does not redesign, so a design's content is settled in the design rooms and in the study groups whose initial proposals they take up. *[proved by l:rooms-plenary-no-redesign, l:rooms-wording-refers, l:rooms-sg-initial]* *[from p:path]* {#p:design-in-rooms}
   1. **Lemma.** Plenary does not actively change a design or hold extended design discussion, which SD-4 says always happens in the design rooms. *[from f:plenary-no-design, f:vehicle, d:rooms]* {#l:rooms-plenary-no-redesign}
   1. **Lemma.** A wording room refers its design questions and suggestions back to the design room. *[from f:wording-refer, f:spec-subgroups, d:rooms]* {#l:rooms-wording-refers}
   1. **Lemma.** A study group's role is to produce an initial proposal that can then enter the usual processing of a design room. *[from f:sg-initial-proposal, d:rooms]* {#l:rooms-sg-initial}
1. **Proposition.** In a subgroup poll each person in the room can cast one vote; ISO bars experts not registered to the working group from its work but lets a convenor invite guests; and a user, who by definition does not participate, casts no vote. *[proved by l:who-room-votes, l:who-registered, l:who-user-no-vote]* {#p:who-votes}
   1. **Lemma.** In a subgroup poll, the votes belong to the people in the room, one vote each. *[from f:one-vote]* {#l:who-room-votes}
   1. **Lemma.** ISO bars experts not registered to a working group from participating in its work, but lets its convenor invite a specific guest to participate in up to two meetings. *[from f:registered, f:guests]* {#l:who-registered}
   1. **Lemma.** A user casts no vote in WG21's polls or in the ballots on WG21's drafts. *[proved by inf:who-voting-participates, inf:who-ballots-members]* *[from d:user]* {#l:who-user-no-vote}
      1. Casting a vote in a subgroup poll or a plenary poll is a way of participating in a WG21 meeting. *[from f:one-vote, f:plenary-consent]* {#inf:who-voting-participates}
      1. The ballots on WG21's drafts are cast by ISO member bodies, among them the national bodies. *[from f:all-nbs-vote, f:iso-ballot, f:pmember-consensus, d:national-body]* {#inf:who-ballots-members}
1. **Proposition.** The players are authors, regulars, study group chairs, design room chairs, the convenor, national bodies, sponsors, and users. The proof takes as given the choices of wording room chairs, of participants who are not regulars, of guests, and of the direction group, and the convenor's appointments, study groups, and guest invitations, and draws no conclusion about them. *[proved by p:chair-decides, p:sponsor-no-vote]* *[from p:path, p:who-votes, d:player, d:author, d:regular, d:rooms, d:national-body, d:sponsor, d:user]* {#m:players}
   1. **Proposition.** In each design room and wording room, whether a proposal advances depends on the chair's consensus call, and in plenary on the convenor's. *[proved by inf:decides-room-consensus, inf:decides-plenary-consensus]* *[from d:rooms]* {#p:chair-decides}
      1. The general consensus on which a design room forwards a design, or a wording room forwards its wording, is determined by the room's chair. *[from f:design-forward, f:wording-forward, l:path-design-forward, l:path-wording-forward, d:rooms]* {#inf:decides-room-consensus}

   1. **Proposition.** Under the quoted rules, a sponsor that is not a liaison organization has no vote of its own: subgroup votes belong to the people in the room, experts appointed by national bodies act in a personal capacity, and ballots are cast by national bodies, while a liaison representative acts for the organization that appointed them. *[from p:who-votes, f:experts-appointed, f:experts-personal, f:iso-ballot, d:sponsor]* {#p:sponsor-no-vote}

Subgroup votes belong to the people in the room, plenary considers the positions of participants, experts appointed by national bodies or category A liaisons act in a personal capacity, and ballots are cast by ISO member bodies, while a liaison representative acts for the organization that appointed them. *[from inf:novote-not-in-room, inf:novote-expert-personal, inf:novote-liaison-acts, inf:novote-plenary-positions, inf:who-ballots-members, l:who-room-votes, d:sponsor, d:participant]* {#p:sponsor-no-vote-wg21 weakened}

In plain terms: a design enters the working draft only after a design room forwards it, a wording room forwards its wording, and plenary approves it, and its content is settled in the design rooms and in the study groups whose initial proposals they take up. Whether a proposal advances depends on the chair's consensus call in each design room and wording room, and on the convenor's in plenary. Subgroup votes belong to the people in the room, a user casts no vote in WG21's polls or in the ballots on its drafts, and a sponsor that is neither an ISO member body nor a liaison organization has no vote of its own in them.
