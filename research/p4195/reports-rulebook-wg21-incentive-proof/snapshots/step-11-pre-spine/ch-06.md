In plain terms: a participant does better attending in person only while attending is worth more to them than the costs that fall on them, which a sponsor's payment lowers, and each participant a sponsor pays to attend adds a vote available to the position they hold. A favorable direction poll makes a design the incumbent, a voter does better voting for a rival only when its advantage outweighs the voter's share of the cost of reversing the incumbent, and each part of a large proposal adopted in parts adds to what a rival to the whole design must replace. The author of a small proposal pays their own costs and, outside SD-4's priority categories, gets agenda time only after the priority work, and each result about large or small proposals holds for any proposal with the attributes it rests on.
