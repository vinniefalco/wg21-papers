## Absence searches

Each search covered the whole of SD-4, or the ISO clauses the item names, using word stems so that other forms match.

- f:no-sustained-procedure: searched sustained, concerned interest, important part, regist, veto, record, minute, assess, address, oppos, objection, objector, dissent, disagree, Against. SD-4 uses "sustained", "concerned interests", and "important part" only in the quoted definition, and names no step for judging or registering an opposition. Holds.
- f:no-merit-ruling: searched rule, ruling, decide, decision, arbit, adjudic, correct, authority, quality, judg, evaluat, assess, merit, opinion, select, pursu, verdict, determin. SD-4 leaves determinations of consensus to chairs and the convenor, gives the design subgroups "selecting and recommending feature designs", and gives the direction group an "opinion"; it assigns no one to rule which side is correct or how good a design is. Holds.
- f:no-paper-objections: searched objection, objector, concern, oppos, dissent, disagree, rebut, critic, counter, defend, answer, address, respon, rationale, revision, feedback. SD-4 asks a paper to show alternatives, asks an author who declines a direction to defend it, and expects revisions "based on feedback"; it asks no proposal paper to state the objections raised against it. Holds.
- f:iso-no-answer: searched respond, response, answer, reply, disposition, address, written, writing, observation across the listed clauses. Only 2.6.5 requires a response ("Committees are required to respond to all comments received", with the secretariat's observations on each). 2.6.4 and 2.5.7 say "address" and 5.5 "addressed" without requiring writing, and the CEO's "comments" in 6.3.3 and 6.4 go with an appeal, not an objection raised in a working group or on a ballot. Holds.
- f:absences: the edition claim matches 0.1 ("This document replaces the ISO/IEC Directives, Part 1:2024, including the ISO, IEC and JTC 1 Supplements."). Holds.
- inf:thm-no-request ("SD-4 never uses the word trade-off"): searched trade-off, tradeoff, trade off. No match. Holds.
- p:disclosure, inf:omit-free, inf:thm-omit-free (drawbacks): searched trade-off, drawback, downside, disadvantage, limitation, weakness, shortcoming, cost, risk. In SD-4 only "shortcomings" (TS feedback), "risk" (TS goals), and "costs" (travel) occur; the TS and white paper asks are why the three findings above limit the absence to proposal papers.
- p:first-part ("SD-4 asks no paper to state those needs"): searched part, parts, later, future, subsequent, extension, depend, preclud, foreclos, follow-on, builds on. No paper, TS or white paper included, is asked what later parts will need. Holds.
- p:same-moves (interests and sponsors): searched disclos, declar, affiliat, employ, sponsor, fund, compan, behalf, interest. In SD-4 "interest" means interest in a topic; "sponsor" and "declar" occur only in ISO 5.3.1 (meeting sponsors), ISO 3.1.2 (a GRS declaration form), and INCITS 3.9.2 (consultant funding). Holds.
- p:integration, inf:thm-fit: searched integrat, together, interact, conflict, consisten, coheren, harmon, fit. SD-4's "integration testing" is the reason to merge a TS early and "fits" belongs to the principle-based paper item; neither is a step that checks separately adopted designs. Holds.
- p:against-costs-more, p:prefer-vs-stop, l:favor-no-risk, inf:doubt-neutral (reasons asked only of Against voters): searched reason, rationale, articulate, explain, justif, why. Only Against voters "may be asked for their reasons by the chair"; the other uses are an objector's own steps in "How to report a potential technical concern". Holds.
- p:record-indistinct, l:iso-register-level: searched record, minute, reason, rationale, justif, why, convenor, convener. Neither SD-4 nor ISO 1.8.2, 2.5.7, or 5.5 requires a record of how an objection was answered or why consensus was called, and 2.5.7 never mentions a convenor. Holds.
- p:sponsor-votes, l:settled-sponsor, l:sponsor-paid-votes, inf:thm-no-cap ("no rule limits how many it pays"): searched the sponsor terms above and read 1.12.1, whose only cap is optional and per P-member or liaison ("The committee may therefore decide upon the maximum number of Experts appointed by each P-member and liaison organization."). Holds.
