## 3. Outcomes
Each poll ends in one of two results.

*Table: What each poll result tells the chair* {#t:outcomes}
| Result | Tells the chair |
|--------|-----------------|
| For | The room leans toward the change |
| Against | The room leans away from the change |
*[from c:advice]*
1. **Lemma.** Either result leaves the decision with the chair. *[from t:outcomes, thm:main]* {#l:either}
1. **Corollary.** The chair decides after every poll. *[from l:either]* {#c:every-poll}
